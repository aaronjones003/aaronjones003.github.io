Health Companion

A personal-use Android companion for capturing meals, nutrition labels, portions and selected health measurements, with review by the owner’s Personal Assistant.

Capture once, review with context

Photograph a meal or nutrition label and describe how much you ate. The assistant reads the evidence, records nutrition with uncertainty, and supports corrections. Permitted Health Connect records add activity, sleep and weight context.

Private synchronization

The phone and Personal Assistant exchange records through the application’s private Google Drive storage. The app requests access only to its own application data, not general Drive files or the owner’s photo library.

This website provides information about the software. The application is for personal use and is not available for public enrollment or download. Nutrition estimates may be incomplete or inaccurate and require review.
