Health Companion

Privacy

Effective September 28, 2026.

Information used

The app stores photos captured in the app, reported amounts and notes, manual weight entries and permitted Health Connect records. Selected sources include Fitbit activity and sleep and Withings weight. Interpreted nutrition can be written to Health Connect when the food log meets its completeness checks.

Processing and synchronization

With the owner’s Google authorization, records and results synchronize through the app’s private Google Drive application-data storage to the owner’s Personal Assistant. Evidence is processed using the owner’s configured OpenAI/Codex service; reviews are delivered through the owner’s configured Slack workflow. These providers process information under their applicable policies and the owner’s accounts. Other nutrition apps can read exported Health Connect records only through permissions the owner grants.

The app requests only its own Drive application-data permission. It does not request general Drive or Google Photos access. Records are not sold or used by this project for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Control and retention

The owner can pause synchronization and revoke Google or Health Connect permissions. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase existing copies. Corrections and deletion markers update the active ledger; original images, earlier exchange revisions, assistant history, messages and backups may remain. Complete erasure currently requires operator cleanup across these locations and provider controls. There is no automatic retention deadline or one-click complete-erasure feature.

This informational website

These pages have no forms, account registration or application analytics. Hosting and network providers may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses. No health records are served by this website.

Contact

The application is intended for its owner. For questions about its Google authorization, use the support address displayed on its Google consent screen.
