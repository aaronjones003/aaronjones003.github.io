Privacy

Effective September 28, 2026.

Health Companion uses information its owner provides or authorizes, including photos, notes and wellness records, for personal tracking and automated analysis.

With permission, it uses private Google Drive application storage for synchronization. It does not access general Drive files or the photo library. Authorized service providers process information to support these features. Records are not sold or used for advertising.

The owner can correct records and revoke access. Revoking access does not delete existing copies; complete deletion requires manual cleanup, including retained history and backups.

Use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

This website contains no health records or forms. Hosting providers may retain ordinary request logs. For privacy questions, use the support address on the app’s Google consent screen.
